Runway Zero — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Runway Zero ("the app") is an air traffic control game for iPhone and iPad. We respect your privacy: the app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars and best scores are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. We do not receive this information.

Game Center

The app uses Apple Game Center for leaderboards. If you are signed in to Game Center, your score is sent to Apple and shown on the leaderboards according to your Game Center settings. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not access your Game Center account details.

Internet connection

The app requires an internet connection to play. It only checks whether your device is online; no data about your connection is collected or sent to us.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
