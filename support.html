Runway Zero — Support

Runway Zero is an air traffic control game: land arrivals, park them, push departures back, taxi to the runway and clear them for takeoff.

Frequently asked questions

The game says "Internet connection required". Runway Zero is an online game. Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data and play resumes where you left off.

My leaderboard score is missing. Make sure you are signed in to Game Center (Settings → Game Center). Scores are submitted when you finish a level.

A plane is stuck. Tap it and choose "Hold position" then "Continue taxi", or switch the departure to another runway.
